Hey, I'm Habiba.

I'm a Graphic Designer and Creative Video Editor who loves transforming ideas into visual experiences.

My work spans social media campaigns, commercial product visuals, interior and ceramic presentation mockups, interactive catalogs, and fast-paced video edits with strong pacing and color polish.

I explore the intersection of design, motion and AI to create visuals that communicate, inspire and stand out — paying close attention to balance, lighting, hierarchy, and emotional rhythm.
